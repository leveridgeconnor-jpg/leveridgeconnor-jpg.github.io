Ascend Hustle — Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026. This page describes Ascend Hustle version 1.0.

Who makes this app

Ascend Hustle is made by Connor Leveridge. Questions about this policy can be sent to levandlev@outlook.com.

What version 1.0 collects

Version 1.0 does not ask for an account, name, or email address. It does not collect usage analytics. It does not include an advertising SDK, an analytics SDK, or a tracking SDK, and it does not ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps. It does not request access to contacts, photos, location, or notifications.

Career data stored on this iPhone

The game keeps one career on the device. That save can include cash, jobs, businesses, employees, homes, mortgages, savings, investments, goals, milestones, happiness, reputation, and weekly history. The developer does not receive a copy of that career.

What leaves the device

Version 1.0 does not send career data, device identifiers, or gameplay events to a server.

How to delete the saved career

Starting a new career replaces the career saved on that iPhone, after the app asks you to confirm. Deleting Ascend Hustle removes the local save. There is no online account to close and no separate copy held by the developer.

If the app changes

This policy describes version 1.0. If a later version adds advertising, analytics, or any other collection, this page will be updated before that version is released. This policy does not say that later versions will work the same way.
